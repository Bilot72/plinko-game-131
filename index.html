How the PLINKO board is structured

The layout of PLINKO is a triangle of pegs, with each row wider than the one above it. When a ball lands on the top peg, it moves left or right at each row, creating a chain of small random steps. That simple design is the reason the plinko game has such a steady rhythm and such clear outcome patterns.

Because the ball can only move in two directions at each peg, most drops collect around the middle pockets and far fewer reach the outer edges. In practical terms, that means common results pay small amounts, while rare edge landings can pay much more. This is the core of how the plinko game works in any plinko casino setting.
